# Major Second

The illustrative ratio is `9/8`. Its equal-tempered comparison is 2 semitone(s). For a root frequency f, the model frequency is f × ratio. The difference in cents is `1200 × log2(ratio) − 100 × 2`.

Two experiment resources use roots of 220 and 440 Hz. The ratio and cents difference stay constant when the root is transposed. This is a mathematical comparison, not proof of a specific historical tuning practice.

Run the browser demo to inspect frequencies, play synthesised tones, and export a WAV file. Tone generation uses a sine wave with short fade-in and fade-out.
